Native Dolby Vision startup handoff
==================================

Code: 59b57fc3c392038700b9447c968be41ab2774c4f

The Generic Kodi integration now commits a valid black DV transport frame
during the native refresh transition, before display-reset releases decoding.
The movie inherits that signal without another HDMI change at its first frame.
The reconstruction library, shaders, movie metadata and packing math are unchanged.
There is no fixed playback delay. Cancellation and a bounded startup timeout
restore ordinary output; normal DV-menu and HDR10 paths retain their policies.
DV_PRIVATE_STARTUP_SIGNAL=0 disables the change for diagnostic A/B comparison.

---------------------------
Same Kodi binary, Saving Private Ryan from 08:00, three starts per configuration,
three seconds between stop and replay, no Kodi restart within each group.
QMS enabled; DV menus disabled; audio passthrough enabled. Both Match Dolby
hardware levels and Quick Sync scaling disabled in both comparison groups.
Asynchronous profiling and synchronous pixel verification disabled.

All commits succeeded. HDMI audio capability loss/recovery still occurred, but
with the new handoff it finished around 0.61-0.63 seconds after opening instead
of 2.12-2.20 seconds, ahead of the first movie frame and clock release.
An earlier prototype incorrectly treated the temporary modeset hint as a
playback-lifetime flag and restored the signal immediately; it was rejected.

Short regression checks passed for P7 and P8.4: clean/controls/controls-closed,
101 unique frames per measured window, no failed/backwards commits, normal
PTS spacing, and pause/seek-while-paused/resume. DV-menu/movie/menu hardware
timings and QMS transition checks passed. Menu settings were restored afterward.
This is bounded software/device qualification, not physical lip-sync or an
independent Dolby pixel-conformance test.

4K59.94 stress sample
--------------------
Gemini Man, Profile 7, from 08:00, both optional settings still disabled:
  Clean:           611 unique frames / 10.18 seconds; zero omissions; max 17 ms.
  Controls open:   337 unique frames /  6.13 seconds; 32 omissions; max 50 ms.
  Controls closed: 364 unique frames /  6.06 seconds; zero omissions; max 17 ms.
The 59.94 Hz output mode and native engine were active. Counts come from actual
successful display-commit PTS, not the less complete Kodi skip counter. Short
wall-clock windows introduce small fps measurement error; they do not imply
the source became exactly 60 fps. Startup also had no missing consecutive PTS.
This sample qualifies clean playback only; 4K59.94 OSD performance remains a
known limitation. It does not qualify an entire movie or all 60 fps content.
